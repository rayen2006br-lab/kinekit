FAQ
Questions?
Can I try before I buy? +
Yes. Try the animations live on this page with your own text, and listen to sound previews from SoundVault 300.
Mac or Windows? +
Both. All Kinekit packs work on macOS and Windows.
Does TextMotion work in Premiere Pro? +
Yes. The panel works in both After Effects (2019 or newer) and Premiere Pro (2021 or newer) — one installer, one license. SoundVault 300 works in any editor: Premiere, Resolve, Final Cut, CapCut and more.
Do I get free updates? +
Yes — updates are free forever. New presets and sounds are added regularly, and you always get them at no extra cost.
What file formats are the sounds? +
WAV, 48 kHz, level-matched and organized in folders by category, with a sound list PDF so you can find what you need fast.
Can I use these commercially? +
Yes. Once purchased, use in any personal or commercial project. No royalties, no attribution needed.
How do I contact support? +
Email you@example.com or DM on Instagram. Response within 24 hours.
